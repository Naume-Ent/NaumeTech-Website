B2C eCommerce

Bringing PPE retail to customers across South Africa.

A Shopify store giving Ambara Safety Solutions a nationwide online sales channel alongside its physical retail operations.

  • B2C eCommerce
  • PPE & safety retail
  • Shopify · Paystack
  • May 2025

The challenge

Ambara previously relied on physical-store sales, making it difficult for customers far from the stores to buy its PPE and safety products.

Our contribution

Naume Tech designed and configured a complete B2C Shopify storefront with product categories, Paystack payments, automated delivery and business-friendly store administration.

What we delivered

  • Shopify storefront design and setup
  • Product catalogue and category configuration
  • Paystack payment gateway integration
  • Automated shipping and delivery workflow
  • SEO and analytics foundations
  • Training, handover and ongoing maintenance

Confirmed outcomes

  • Customers can place orders beyond the physical-store area
  • The online store has received real customer orders
  • Payment and delivery processes are automated
  • The client can independently manage products and orders
  • Naume Tech continues supporting the platform

Outcomes listed here are based on confirmed operational experience; no unverified revenue, conversion-rate or performance figures are claimed.
