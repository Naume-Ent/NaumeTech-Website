Managed IT & Microsoft 365

An ongoing IT partnership supporting everyday business operations.

Outsourced IT support for Mbokoto Mining Group covering Microsoft 365 administration, employee helpdesk, device and connectivity support, and business technology assistance.

  • Managed IT & Microsoft 365
  • Mining
  • Microsoft 365 · IT helpdesk

The challenge

Mbokoto needed dependable external IT expertise to manage Microsoft 365 and help employees resolve daily technology challenges.

Our contribution

Through a monthly retainer, Naume Tech provides scoped Microsoft 365 administration and day-to-day IT assistance spanning email, user access, devices, networking, security configuration, backups and vendor coordination.

What we delivered

  • Microsoft 365 tenants, licensing, Exchange and Outlook
  • Teams, OneDrive and SharePoint support
  • Employee IT helpdesk and device troubleshooting
  • Onboarding, offboarding, access and MFA configuration
  • Network connectivity and backup administration
  • On-site assistance and IT vendor coordination

Confirmed outcomes

  • Staff support issues have been resolved
  • Microsoft 365 accounts, licences and business email have been administered
  • Employee onboarding and offboarding have been completed
  • MFA and access-security settings have been implemented
  • Device, connectivity and backup administration tasks have been supported
  • On-site technical assistance and an ongoing support partnership are in place

Outcomes listed here are based on confirmed operational experience; no unverified revenue, conversion-rate or performance figures are claimed.
