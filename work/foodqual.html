Multi-vendor marketplace

A digital marketplace where farmers manage their own products.

A Shopify-powered multi-vendor marketplace that helps smallholder farmers connect with customers through individual vendor dashboards and central administration.

  • Multi-vendor marketplace
  • Agriculture & food retail
  • Shopify · Paystack
  • August 2026

The challenge

FoodQual's mission is to create a more accessible route to market for smallholder farmers. The business needed more than a single-seller online store: farmers required individual tools to list and manage products while operators maintained control over the marketplace.

Our contribution

Naume Tech implemented a Shopify commerce experience with vendor registration and approval, individual dashboards, inventory and pricing management, ordering, commissions and payouts, Paystack payments and automated delivery.

What we delivered

  • Farmer/vendor registration and administrator approval
  • Individual vendor dashboards, listings, inventory and prices
  • Vendor order management and notifications
  • Marketplace commissions and payouts
  • Paystack payment gateway
  • Automated delivery and central reporting

Confirmed outcomes

  • Farmers have registered, been approved and listed products
  • Customers have placed successful orders and payments
  • Automated delivery has supported real orders
  • Vendor commissions or payouts have been processed
  • The FoodQual team independently manages the marketplace
  • Naume Tech provides ongoing support

Outcomes listed here are based on confirmed operational experience; no unverified revenue, conversion-rate or performance figures are claimed.
