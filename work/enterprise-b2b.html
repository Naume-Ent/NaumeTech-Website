Enterprise eCommerce

Supporting enterprise B2B commerce across African markets.

Naume Tech contributed full-stack engineering to a Coca-Cola B2B eCommerce initiative through an IT delivery partner, working within a broader engineering team.

  • Enterprise eCommerce
  • FMCG & distribution
  • Adobe Commerce / Magento 2
  • Completed engagement

The challenge

The wider initiative involved complex enterprise B2B requirements such as company accounts, business pricing, ordering and enterprise-system integrations across multiple markets.

Our contribution

Under a subcontract through an IT partner, Naume Tech contributed Magento 2 modules, REST APIs, B2B accounts and pricing, checkout, integrations, frontend development, testing, performance work and production support.

What we delivered

  • Magento 2 custom modules and full-stack development
  • Company accounts, product catalogue and B2B pricing
  • Cart, checkout, ordering and payment integration work
  • REST APIs and ERP/order-management integrations
  • Frontend/storefront enhancement
  • Automated tests, QA, troubleshooting and maintenance

Confirmed outcomes

  • The wider platform supports live B2B ordering
  • ERP and order-management integrations have operated in production
  • Company-account pricing and payment capabilities are operational
  • Technical defects have been resolved and QA improved
  • The wider initiative operated in South Africa, Botswana, Kenya, Namibia, Uganda, Mozambique and Tanzania

Engagement attribution: Naume Tech contributed through an IT delivery partner; it was not directly contracted by the end client and was not the sole platform implementer. Client and partner logos, platform screenshots and confidential technical information are intentionally excluded.

Outcomes listed here are based on confirmed operational experience; no unverified revenue, conversion-rate or performance figures are claimed.
