How we work

Defined scope. Clear next steps.

We start by understanding the business problem, then agree on a realistic scope, responsibilities and approach before work begins.

Support availability, on-site visits, third-party costs and response targets are agreed in each proposal. No blanket 24/7 coverage is implied.

What an engagement may include

  • Initial review of your users, devices and support priorities
  • Agreed support scope, contact process and escalation arrangements
  • Remote support and scheduled maintenance within the contracted scope
  • Periodic reviews of recurring issues and improvement opportunities

Frequently asked questions

Good questions, clear answers.

Can you support a business with no internal IT department?

Yes. We can discuss a defined remote support arrangement based on the size of your team and the systems you use.

Are site visits and emergency support included?

These depend on the agreed service package. We will make hours, response targets and any additional charges clear in the proposal.

Can you also look after our Microsoft 365 environment?

Yes. Microsoft 365 administration can be scoped alongside managed IT support or as a separate service.
