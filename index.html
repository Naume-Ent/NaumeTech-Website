About Naume Tech

Naume Tech is a black youth-owned technology company driven by innovation, inclusion, and impact. We empower businesses through smart, scalable, and forward-thinking tech solutions. Founded by ambitious young professionals, our mission is to bridge digital gaps and build future-ready systems for clients across industries. We combine creativity, technical expertise, and a deep understanding of local challenges to deliver tailored IT services that make a difference. We're not just building software - we're building the future.
